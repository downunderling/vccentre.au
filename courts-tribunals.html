Courts & Tribunals

The Video Conferencing Centre provides remote video-link rooms for court and tribunal hearings. In addition we provide interconnecting services for people to use a laptop or smartphone to connect through to court hearings.

Services are provided to all jurisdictions for both civil and criminal matters.
